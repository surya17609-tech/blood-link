01. OUR PURPOSE

Building a clear, consent-based donor platform

Blood Link aims to connect people willing to donate blood with people searching for donors. In an emergency, finding the right donor should not depend on forwarded messages and endless phone calls. We are building one simple, consent-based place where a verified request can quickly reach nearby, willing donors — privately and securely.

02. OUR STORY

Why we created Blood Link

During an emergency, families contact people one by one through calls, WhatsApp groups, and social media. This takes time, creates confusion, and can expose private medical information. Donor details are scattered, availability is outdated, and there is no clear way to track if a request is open, matched, or completed.

Blood Link is our attempt to solve this with a structured workflow. We are a dedicated student team building a solution to make emergency donor coordination humane, organized, and efficient.

OUR MISSION

Easier & Private

Make finding potential blood donors easier, while protecting privacy and reducing fraudulent requests.

OUR VISION

Connected Network

A connected community ready to help — where no family feels alone when searching for a donor.

OUR VALUES

Core Principles

Trust, Privacy, Kindness, and Accessibility. We support coordination. Hospitals remain responsible for eligibility and transfusion decisions.
